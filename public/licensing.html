Media boundary

Original panoramas, extracted sounds, vector and Flash-derived files, and branding are retained as source studies. The six scene remasters are AI-assisted interpretations, and the film is a new silent scenic study assembled from those interpretations. They are labeled as homage material and are not presented as official recovered V5 assets.
